Help us test Combat Wombat

A fast combat tracker for 5e tabletop games: initiative, hit points, conditions, spells, a Combat Monitor for the TV and a view for players' phones. This is the public beta, so expect rough edges and tell us what you find.

What to try

  • Run a few rounds: Start combat, Next turn, damage, healing and Undo.
  • The Target window: a Fireball on the goblins, with saves.
  • Conditions, concentration and death saves.
  • Open the Combat Monitor in a second window, or on a TV.
  • Show “Roll for initiative” before combat.
  • The Library: add creatures, NPCs and spells.
  • Different skins in Settings.
  • On a phone or tablet, as well as a computer.

Known limits in this beta

  • No real sign-in or syncing yet: everything stays in this browser.
  • Players' phones only connect to a table open in the same browser, so test them in a second tab or window for now.
  • Subscribe doesn't take payments yet.
  • Least tested on Safari and iPhone: reports from there are especially welcome.

Found something?

Open an issue on GitHub with what you did, what you expected and what happened. Mention the version ({{VERSION}}), the test account, and your browser and device. A screenshot helps a lot.
