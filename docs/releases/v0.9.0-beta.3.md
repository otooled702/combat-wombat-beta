Third beta of Combat Wombat: fixes and improvements from playtesting, mostly on phones.

**Changes**
- Cards open in Focus by default: only the card in turn is open. Before combat every card starts closed; tap one to open it. Full still opens them all.
- The top bar has a Start combat button before combat starts.
- "Roll for initiative" no longer shows a stale or half-drawn label; it keeps the same size on and off.
- The timer can time each turn or the whole combat (a whole-combat countdown is set in minutes).
- Groups: conditions can go on all members or on chosen ones (Prone on goblins 2 and 3). Each member shows its own conditions and can lose one on its own. "+1" is now an "Add one" button.
- "Save" on creature cards is now "Saving throw".
- The Roll for initiative d20 has even lines.

**Phones and tablets**
- Buttons no longer stay highlighted after you tap them.
- The Full screen button is hidden where the browser can't go full screen (iPhone).
- The top bar fits on one or two rows; on a phone on its side it scrolls away instead of covering the cards.
- The Combat Monitor preview is a row of small cards you swipe sideways, with its close button above them.
- The Library: search, sort and "When adding" take much less room, and the whole window scrolls.
- The Target window: one scrolling area with Apply pinned at the bottom; nothing overlaps.
- On a narrow card, the amount box sits beside both the Damage and the Heal buttons.

**Known limits** (unchanged)
- No real sign-in or syncing: everything stays in the browser.
- Player phones only connect within the same browser in this beta.
- Subscribe doesn't take payments.
- Not yet tested on real Safari/iPhone hardware.
