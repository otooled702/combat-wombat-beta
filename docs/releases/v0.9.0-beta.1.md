First public beta of Combat Wombat. Try it on the live preview; the files below are the same builds.

**Files**
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.1-beta.html`: the beta app, with test accounts.
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.1-demo.html`: the public demo.
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.1.html`: the app on its own, no test accounts.

**Test accounts** (no passwords; they live only in your browser)
- Sam Rivera, yearly plan: a campaign under way.
- Riley Chen, monthly plan: a brand-new, empty table.
- Casey Moreno, no subscription: the demo, with the tour and the Subscribe window.

**What's in it**
- Initiative, turns, hit points, conditions, concentration, death saves and groups.
- The Target window: damage, saves, attacks, spells (including healing) and conditions on many creatures at once.
- The Combat Monitor for a TV, "Roll for initiative!", and the player phone view.
- 331 SRD creatures, standard NPCs and 339 SRD spells.
- 13 skins, including the new Watercolor; every text at 4.5:1 contrast.

**Known limits**
- No real sign-in or syncing: everything stays in the browser.
- Player phones only connect within the same browser in this beta.
- Subscribe doesn't take payments.
- Least tested on Safari and iPhone.

Report bugs with the "Bug report" issue form.
