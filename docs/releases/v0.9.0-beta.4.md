Fourth beta of Combat Wombat: your own spells, and spells sorted by what they do.

**Changes**
- Library > Spells: make your own spell with **+ New spell**, or **Edit a copy** of any SRD spell. The SRD original is never changed; your copy is listed under **Yours**, where you can edit or delete it (Delete can be undone).
- The spell form covers level, school, casting time, range, duration, concentration, classes, an attack roll or a saving throw, one damage type, one condition, healing, and extra dice per slot level (cantrips grow at levels 5, 11 and 17).
- Spells have tags: Damage, Healing, Condition and Utility. Tags come from what a spell does, so they need no upkeep. The Library filters by tag.
- The Target window's Spells tab has the same chips (All, Damage, Healing, Conditions). All hides utility spells. This replaces the old "in combat" ticks in the Library.
- Your spells are kept with the table and work in the Target window like SRD spells, including casting at a higher level.

**Fixes**
- Undo from the Library (for example after deleting a spell) now updates the open Library window.
- Target window rows no longer stretch tall when only a few creatures are listed.
- Target window > Spells: the search box is no longer squeezed to a sliver beside the level menu, and the spell list fills the column down to Apply instead of stopping halfway.

**Known limits**
- Your own spells take one damage type and one condition.
- Not yet tested on Safari, iPhone or real phones.
