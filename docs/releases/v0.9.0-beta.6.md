Sixth beta of Combat Wombat: spell descriptions, and the spell list on phones.

**Changes**
- Every spell has its rules text under **Description**: casting time, range, components, duration, what it does (for example, Aid: "Choose up to three creatures within range"), and what a higher-level slot or a cantrip upgrade adds. Tables (Confusion, Control Weather, Divine Word…) show as tables.
- Description is in the Library's spell list and on the spell card in the Target window. Once opened in the Target window it stays open from spell to spell.
- Your own spells can have components, a description and higher-level text; Edit a copy brings the original's text along.

**Fixes**
- Library > Spells on phones: the rows were jumbled (the summary squeezed into a sliver on the left, the name pushed right under the edit button). Each row now reads name, details, tags, summary, Description, with the edit button at the top right.

**Notes**
- The descriptions add about 230 KB, so the app file is now about 1.3 MB.
- Not yet tested on Safari, iPhone or real phones.
