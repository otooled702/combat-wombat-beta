Fifth beta of Combat Wombat: fixes to spells from playtesting.

**Changes**
- Target window > Spells: **All** now lists every spell, utility spells included, and the list is no longer cut off at 80. A **Utility** chip joins All, Damage, Healing and Conditions.
- **Aid** is a healing spell: it raises each target's maximum and current hit points by 5 (+5 per slot level above 2), so it works on creatures already at full health. The amount fills itself in; one Undo reverses it.
- **Regenerate** heals 4d8 + 15.
- Your own spells can also raise maximum hit points ("Also raises maximum hit points (like Aid)").

**Fixes**
- The spell form in the Library could not scroll, so Save and Cancel were out of reach on shorter screens. The form now scrolls and Save and Cancel stay pinned at the bottom.

**Known limits**
- Aid's higher maximum stays until you change it or undo it (the spell lasts 8 hours, longer than a fight). For players it also shows on the Party list.
- Not yet tested on Safari, iPhone or real phones.
