Second beta of Combat Wombat.

**Changes**
- New logo: the wombat mark and the COMBAT WOMBAT wordmark, everywhere the name appears (menu, empty screen, Combat Monitor, player phones, sign-in screen and the testers' page).
- Every public screen is now in the Color Spray look: the testers' page, the sign-in screen, the demo, the tour and the Subscribe window.
- The Watercolor skin has been removed. A table saved with Watercolor opens in Daylight. There are now 12 skins.
- Two handwriting fonts that only Watercolor used are no longer loaded.

**Files**
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.2-beta.html`: the beta app, with test accounts.
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.2-demo.html`: the public demo.
- `combat-wombat-v0.9.0-beta.2.html`: the app on its own, no test accounts.

**Known limits** (unchanged)
- No real sign-in or syncing: everything stays in the browser.
- Player phones only connect within the same browser in this beta.
- Subscribe doesn't take payments.
- Least tested on Safari and iPhone.
